# Account and privacy

THUMDER is used **without an account**: you come in as a guest and your files are kept in the browser or on the device. An account is only for one thing: having the same files on all your devices.

## Sign in

1. In the side menu, open **Profile** (**Debug** group) and press **Sign in to synchronise**.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or create an account with **Register**.

The ways to sign in depend on where you use THUMDER:

| Where | Ways to sign in |
| :-- | :-- |
| Browser, also on a phone | Email and password, Google or GitHub |
| Desktop application | Email and password |
| Android application | Email and password; Google and GitHub, when the version includes them |

Forgot your password? **I forgot my password**, on the sign-in screen, sends you an email to change it.

## Synchronisation

While you are signed in, your files are kept in the cloud and on the device, and they synchronise by themselves. The **Profile** says how it goes: **Synchronising**, **Up to date** or **Pending (no connection)**. Without a connection you can keep working: the changes go up when it comes back. **Synchronise now** forces it.

The files you had as a guest join your account when you sign in. When you **sign out**, what is missing goes up to the account and the files leave the device, so that on a shared computer the next person does not see them.

### What is kept in the account and what is not

| In the account | Only on the device |
| :-- | :-- |
| Your files and folders | The settings of the simulator |
| Your profile (email and name) | Your progress in the exercises |
| | The theme and the language |

## Delete the account

In **Profile**, press **Delete the account** and confirm it. Your account and all its files are deleted, in the cloud and on that device, and it cannot be undone: download first what you want to keep from the file manager.

For your security, it can only be deleted for a few minutes after signing in. If it has been longer, THUMDER asks you to sign in again.

What is deleted, what is not and how to ask for it if you cannot get in: [Delete your account](https://thumder.netlify.app/landing/delete-account).

## Your privacy

The first time, THUMDER asks what you allow. What it needs to work (your files, the settings, the language and the theme) is kept on your device and does not leave it. The rest is optional:

- **Analytics**: Google Analytics counts, in aggregate, the visits and the views used. It uses cookies.
- **Error reports**: if THUMDER fails, it sends the error, the view, the version, the browser and the language, without your account or your files. It uses no cookies.

You can change your choice whenever you want with **Privacy settings**, at the bottom of every page. All the details are in the [privacy policy](https://thumder.netlify.app/landing/privacy).
