# Delete your THUMDER account

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

THUMDER is the DLX processor simulator by Antonio Mudarra Machuca, on the web, on the desktop and on Android and iOS. It can be used without an account. If you created one, this page explains how to delete it and what is deleted.

## From THUMDER

The same on the web (thumder.netlify.app), in the desktop application and in the Android and iOS applications:

1. Sign in with your account.
2. Open the side menu and, under **Account**, press **Profile**.
3. Press **Delete the account** and confirm it.

For your security, an account can only be deleted for a few minutes after signing in. If it has been longer, THUMDER asks you to sign in again: do it and delete it right after.

## If you cannot get in

Write to [nonodev96@gmail.com](mailto:nonodev96@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20THUMDER%20account) from the email of your account, with the subject "Delete my THUMDER account". It will be deleted within a month at most, and you will get an email when it is done.

## What is deleted

- **Your account**: the email, the name and the photo Firebase Authentication identifies you with, and the way you sign in (password, Google, GitHub or Apple).
- **Your profile**, with the same data, in Cloud Firestore.
- **All your files** kept in the cloud.
- **The copy of your files on the device** where you delete the account.

Everything is deleted at once and cannot be recovered. If you want to keep a program, export it first from the file manager.

## What is not deleted

- **What is not linked to your account**, because there is no way to know it is yours: the error reports, which do not carry your account and are kept until the error is fixed, and the Google Analytics statistics, if you accepted them, which are aggregated.
- **The copy of your files on other devices** where you were signed in. That session stops working, but the files stay on that device until you delete the THUMDER data there: in the browser, the data of the site; on Android and iOS, by uninstalling the application.
- **What is kept on the device and not in the account**: your progress in the exercises, the configuration of the simulator, the theme and the language. They go with the THUMDER data of that device.

More details in the privacy policy.
